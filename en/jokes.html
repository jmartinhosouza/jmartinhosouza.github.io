Jokes

Some of these jokes came out of my own mind; the others I simply found too good not to share. Go ahead and tell them to your friends — just don't blame me if any of them die of laughter. :)

A conversation between two friends:
— What did you get on your IQ test?
— A 10. And mind you, I didn't even study!

I discovered this joke through the fortune command.

Q: How many elephants can you fit into a VW Beetle?
A: Four. Two in the front and two in the back.

Q: How can you tell there’s an elephant in your refrigerator?
A: There's a trail of footprints on the butter.

Q: How can you tell there are two elephants in your refrigerator?
A: There are two trails of footprints on the butter.

Q: How can you tell there are three elephants in your refrigerator?
A: The door won't close.

Q: How can you tell there are four elephants in your refrigerator?
A: Look for a VW Beetle parked outside.
